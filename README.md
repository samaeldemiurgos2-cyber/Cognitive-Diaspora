# Cognitive Diaspora

Cognitive Diaspora is a public research portfolio about context, orientation,
traversal, provenance, and the design of long-lived agentic systems.

Its working thesis is:

> **Context is topology. Orientation is the primitive. Traversal replaces compaction.**

This repository is a curated public research snapshot. It contains research
propositions, experimental designs, and safe conceptual artifacts—not the
private working environment, operational implementation, or conversation
archive. No sessions or transcripts are published here.

## Epistemic status

The material here distinguishes working abstractions, hypotheses, planned
experiments, and observed results. The dated experiment is a design, not a
measurement report. VERSO is a conceptual framework; it is not presented as a
completed implementation or a claim about human neuroscience.
